## Design Context

### Users
- **Who**: Data analysts, BI engineers, data scientists, and developers.
- **Context**: Performing fast, local-first dataset profiling, anomaly cleaning, and SQL playground operations.
- **Job to be Done**: Identify data inconsistencies (nulls, duplicates, bad types) and extract insights via SQL/NLP without uploading data to external servers.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone**: Clinical, efficient, fast, precise.
- **3-Word Personality**: Secure, Dense, Fluid.
- **Emotional Goals**: Control, clarity, confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Premium Cockpit. Clear hierarchies, compact spacing (Density 8), and smooth micro-interactions (Intensity 6).
- **Theme**: High-contrast Charcoal Dark.
- **Anti-References**: Cheap AI "blue-purple" neon gradients, glowing cards, generic Unsplash faces, and side-stripe colored borders.

### Design Principles
1. **Local-First Speed**: Make interaction states instantaneous and tactile (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
2. **Material Refraction**: Use thin borders (`border-white/10` or `border-outline-variant/60`) and frosted glass surfaces for elevation.
3. **Data Density**: Monospace typography for numerical indicators. Keep dashboards compact and informative without boxing elements in heavy cards.
