# Brand — Nexora Analytics

_Status: active_

Nexora is a local-first precision data intelligence and profiling sandbox running client-side inside the user's browser.

## Tone & Voice
- **Precise**: Clinical and clear explanations. No fluff or boilerplate text.
- **Fast**: Immediate client-side operations should be highlighted.
- **Trustworthy**: Secure local-first sandbox environment.

## Design Tokens & Theme
- **Theme**: Off-black and charcoal base (`#0b1326`) with Slate (`#dae2fd`) foreground.
- **Accent**: Electric Blue/Primary (`#c0c1ff`) with subtle high-contrast accents (Emerald `#34d399` for success, Amber `#f59e0b` for warnings).
- **Typography**: Display/Headlines in Geist Sans (`--font-geist`). Monospace/Numbers in Geist Mono or JetBrains Mono (`--font-jetbrains-mono`).
- **Glow & Depth**: Thin 1px inner borders with frosted glass backgrounds. No heavy neon AI purple button glows.
